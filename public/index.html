Model basis and assumptions

This is a kinematic teaching model. Immediate-roof fragments cave after the shields pass; the main roof bends, separates and shears above them. Fragment contact and gas flow are approximations. The slotted completion is a conceptual example requested for this view, not a documented completion of RGGS 12-12-04.

The model spans 52 m of face advance over an assumed 12-hour mining interval. The illustrative bore has a 0.20 m internal diameter, a dominant gas inlet 1.20 m above its bottom, and 14 kPa of available gas-driving pressure. Casing width is visually enlarged; the full overburden between this cut interval and the surface wellhead is omitted. Freshwater density is 1,000 kg/m³. Inlet backpressure is ρg times water height above that inlet; water below it does not impose a full liquid head on incoming gas. Inflow, carrying capacity, beds and production are uncalibrated by default.

Process mode Illustrative (teaching) keeps those scrub-cleared defaults. Calibrated ranges (literature) swaps labeled engineering defaults: 30 kPa drive (typical active gob-ventilation borehole vacuum in published CFD baselines) and stronger connected-water supply rates. The √(1−head/drive) restriction law is retained in both modes as an orifice/Bernoulli teaching form. Calibrated values are transparent literature-range picks — not Warrior Met / Blue Creek field calibration or mine-specific accuracy.

Warrior Met Coal Gas’s Blue Creek gob unit is documented in Alabama’s February 2019 hearing results, Order 2019-09. EPA’s 1994 report describes Black Warrior Methane’s conventional vertical CBM wells, surface gob wells and in-mine boreholes. These sources provide regional context, not site-specific hydraulic inputs or company endorsement.
